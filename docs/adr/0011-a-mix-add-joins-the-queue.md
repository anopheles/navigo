# A mix-add joins the queue, and the listener picks where

A Mix replaces the queue ([ADR 0006](0006-mix-is-picked-from-the-cache.md)), which is too much when the listener only wants more of something. A mix-add picks tracks the way a Mix does and adds them, to the end (`gM`) or after the current track (`gN`), as `A` and `a` do. We chose two commands over a config setting so the choice is made at each use and a key means the same thing everywhere, and over `gMa`/`gMA` to keep it to two keys after a count.

It differs from a Mix where keeping the queue changes what makes sense. It leaves out tracks already queued or played this session, and a seed track itself, since the listener asked for more of a kind and `A` already adds that track. It defaults to 10 tracks, the radio's batch, rather than a Mix's 50. A pending one survives a change to the queue or what plays, because adding takes nothing away, while a Mix is dropped because replacing the queue then would be a surprise. It stays one-shot and is one undo step. Unlike the radio's additions ([ADR 0010](0010-the-radio-tops-up-the-queue-and-steering-is-session-only.md)), its tracks are the listener's own edit, so they are undoable, carry no mark, and ignore radio steering.
